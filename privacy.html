Who we are

Capital Refurbishment Solutions ("we", "us", "our") provides commercial fit-out services in London and the surrounding area. You can contact us on 07863 783993.

What we collect

When you complete the enquiry form on this website we collect the information you give us. That usually includes your name, phone number, email address and details about your property and your project.

We also collect some technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the advertising campaign that brought you here (for example UTM parameters and click identifiers such as fbclid).

Why we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry by phone, text message or email.
  • To prepare a quote and discuss your project.
  • To measure how well our advertising works.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Our lawful basis for contacting you about your enquiry is our legitimate interest in responding to a request you made, and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who we share it with

We share your information with service providers who help us run our business. These currently include our customer relationship management provider (HighLevel), our website and analytics providers, and advertising platforms such as Meta. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to measure advertising performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Turning off cookies may stop parts of the site working properly.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we need them to deal with your project and to meet our legal obligations, and then we delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to how we use it, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
